Your transfer key

On the other device, open the pet's menu → Move to another device → Import, or use "Have a transfer key?" in setup. It holds your names, level and stats, look, and settings. Sprite pack images aren't included: export packs as zips.

Import a transfer key

Paste the key from your other device. This replaces your names, level, stats, and settings here (a backup of the stats is kept).
